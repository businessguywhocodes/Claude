# Showcase: Real Artifacts People Have Built

**Extras · Real-world examples · ~20 minutes of clicking around**

Module 4 explained Artifacts. This page shows what people actually build with them. Every example below was made with Claude and published as a public artifact. Open a few, use them, and notice how small and specific most of them are. **That's the lesson: the best artifacts solve one concrete problem.**

> **How to use this page.** Click through and try each tool. Most public artifacts have an option to customise or remix them, which opens a copy in your own Claude chat so you can change it ("make this a housekeeping checklist instead", "use rupees", "add our brand colours"). Links were collected in October 2026 from public sources (listed at the bottom). Creators can unpublish their artifacts at any time, so a link may stop working.

## Notable project: The Way of Code

[The Way of Code](https://www.thewayofcode.com/), by music producer Rick Rubin with Anthropic, pairs 81 short meditations with interactive artifacts. You can modify any of them by describing a change. It's a good example of artifacts used for creative, not practical, work.

## Patterns to steal

Looking across these examples, a few patterns repeat:

1. **Tracker:** log something daily and see trends (habits, macros, maintenance, complaints).
2. **Calculator:** inputs, a formula, a clear answer (ROI, pricing, buy vs. rent, budgets).
3. **Dashboard:** upload a CSV and get charts, filters and rankings.
4. **Generator:** one click, fresh ideas (names, scenarios, icebreakers).
5. **Trainer:** quizzes, flashcards and role-play scenarios.
6. **Planner:** a structured, printable plan (trips, events, menus).
7. **AI helper:** paste something in and get a tailored result back (translator, optimiser, concierge).

## Try it

1. **Remix one.** Open an example close to your job, use its customise or remix option, and adapt it. For example, turn the Hotel Booking Game into front-desk training scenarios for your property.
2. **Build a tracker or calculator from scratch.** Pick a pattern above and describe the one you'd actually use this week.
3. **Publish and share.** Publish your artifact and send the link to one friend or colleague. (Check what's in it first: published artifacts can be viewed by anyone with the link.)
