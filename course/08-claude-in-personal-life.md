# Module 8: Claude in Your Personal Life

**Track B · Everyday leverage · ~25 minutes**

Claude is just as useful outside work. This module is a set of plays: situations where it reliably helps, with a starter prompt for each.

## Planning and logistics

**Trip planning**
> Plan a 5-day trip to Sri Lanka in December for two adults who like food, short hikes, and beaches but hate long drives (max 3 hours a day). Mid-range budget, about ₹1.5 lakh total excluding flights. Give a day-by-day plan, where to stay each night, and what to book in advance. Ask me questions first.

**Events**
> Help me plan my parents' 35th anniversary dinner for 20 people at home. Create a timeline for the 2 weeks before, a shopping list, a menu with 2 vegetarian and 1 Jain-friendly option, and a task split for me and my two siblings.

**Weekly routines**
> Here's what my week looks like [paste]. Help me design a realistic weekly routine that includes 3 workouts, 2 evenings with no screens, and time for my side project. Be realistic. I usually overcommit.

## Money (understand, then decide)

> Explain the difference between the old and new income tax regimes in India for a salaried person earning ₹18 lakh with a home loan. Use a table. Tell me what documents and numbers I should take to my CA to decide.

> Here's my last 3 months of spending, exported from my bank app (CSV). Categorise it, show where the money goes, and suggest 3 realistic changes. Don't lecture me.

*Remember: Claude helps you understand. For actual tax filing and investment decisions, confirm with a qualified professional.*

## Health and wellbeing (prepare, don't diagnose)

> I have a doctor's appointment about recurring migraines. Help me prepare: what information should I track for the next two weeks, and what questions should I ask the doctor?

> Translate this lab report into plain English. What do the flagged values mean in general, and what should I ask my doctor about? [attach]

> Create a 4-week beginner running plan for someone who can currently jog 10 minutes. 3 days a week, and I have a sensitive knee.

## Learning anything

Claude is a patient, always-available tutor.

> Teach me the basics of investing in index funds. Start by asking what I already know. Explain one concept at a time, check my understanding with a question, and only move on when I get it right.

> I'm learning Spanish (beginner). Have a simple conversation with me about ordering food. Correct my mistakes gently after each message.

> Explain how airline pricing works like I'm smart but have no background in it. Then give me 3 practical tips for booking flights.

## Writing and communication

**Hard conversations**
> I need to tell my landlord I'm moving out 2 months earlier than the lease says. Help me write a message that's polite, firm, and proposes a solution (I'll help find a new tenant). Then help me prepare for the likely pushback.

**Messages that matter**
> Help me write a heartfelt wedding speech for my best friend. Ask me for 5 stories or details first, then draft a 3-minute speech that's funny but ends warmly.

**Admin**
> Draft a complaint to my broadband provider. Outages on these dates [list], 3 support tickets with no fix. I want a bill credit and a technician visit within 48 hours.

## Decisions

> I'm deciding between two job offers [details]. Help me think it through: ask about what I value most, then build a weighted decision matrix. After that, argue for the option I'm *not* leaning toward.

> We're thinking of buying a car vs. continuing with cabs. Here's our usage [details]. Do a 5-year cost comparison, and list the non-financial factors.

## Home and family

- **Cooking:** "Here's what's in my fridge [photo]. Give me 3 dinner options under 30 minutes."
- **Kids:** "My 8-year-old asked why the sky is blue. Help me explain it with an experiment we can do at home."
- **Fixing things:** "Here's a photo of my leaking tap. What's probably wrong, and can I fix it myself?"
- **Gifts:** "Gift ideas for my father (62, loves gardening and old Hindi films), budget ₹5,000."

## Make it a habit

- Keep the **mobile app** handy. Voice mode is great for thinking out loud on walks.
- Create a few **personal projects** (Module 6) so Claude knows your family, preferences, and goals.
- Use **web search** for anything with prices, opening hours, or rules.

## Try it

Pick three plays from this module that match something in your life *this week* and run them for real.

## Key takeaways

- Claude helps across planning, money, health, learning, writing, decisions, and home.
- For money, health, and law: use Claude to understand and prepare, then confirm with a professional.
- Asking Claude to ask you questions first makes personal plans fit your life.
