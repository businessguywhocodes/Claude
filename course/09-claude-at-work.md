# Module 9: Claude at Work

**Track B · Everyday leverage · ~30 minutes**

This module is a playbook by role, with examples from hotels, travel, and startups. Find your role, try the plays, and steal from the other roles too.

## The universal plays (everyone)

| Play | Starter prompt |
|---|---|
| **Inbox triage** | "Here are 12 emails [paste]. Group them into: needs reply today, can wait, FYI only. Draft replies for the first group." |
| **Meeting prep** | "I'm meeting [person/company] about [topic]. Here's what I know [paste]. Give me the 3 goals I should have, likely objections, and 5 good questions." |
| **Meeting notes to actions** | "Here's the transcript [paste]. Give me decisions, action items with owners and dates, and open questions." |
| **Write it shorter** | "Cut this to half the length without losing any decision or number." |
| **Explain it to me** | "Explain [concept/document] to me like I'm new to the industry, then quiz me with 3 questions." |
| **Get unstuck** | "I'm stuck on [problem]. Ask me 5 questions to clarify it, then suggest 3 approaches." |
| **Second opinion** | "Review this [plan/doc] as a sceptical senior colleague. What would you push back on?" |

## Operations

- **SOPs**: "Turn this messy description of our check-in process [paste] into a clear SOP with steps, owners, and what to do when things go wrong."
- **Checklists**: "Create a pre-arrival checklist for VIP guests at a 60-room business hotel."
- **Incident reviews**: "Here's what happened during last night's overbooking [notes]. Write a blameless incident review: timeline, root cause, what went well, what to change."
- **Vendor comparison**: "Compare these 3 laundry vendor quotes [attach] in a table and flag hidden costs."
- **Staff training**: "Create a 15-minute training session for front-desk staff on handling late check-out requests, with 3 role-play scenarios."

## Guest experience and support

- **Reply drafting**: use a **Project** with your policies and brand voice (Module 6) so every draft is on-policy.
- **Review analysis**: "Here are 200 recent reviews [CSV]. What are the top 5 praise themes and top 5 complaint themes, with example quotes and rough frequency? What should we fix first?"
- **Review responses**: "Draft responses to these 5 reviews. Thank, acknowledge specifics, don't be defensive, invite them back. Under 80 words each."
- **Multilingual**: "Translate this pre-arrival message into Hindi, Tamil, German, and French, keeping the warm tone."
- **FAQ building**: "From these 50 guest questions [paste], build an FAQ page with clear, short answers using our policies [attach]."

## Sales and partnerships

- **Prospect research**: "Research [hotel/group] with web search: size, locations, recent news, likely priorities, and who to contact. Then suggest an angle for our outreach."
- **Outreach**: "Write a 3-email sequence to independent hotel owners about [product]. Plain language, one clear ask per email, no hype."
- **Objection handling**: "Here are the 10 objections we hear most [list]. Write a short, honest response to each, and when to walk away."
- **Proposal drafting**: "Using this template and these call notes, draft a proposal for [client]."
- **Call debrief**: "Here are my call notes. Update the deal summary: needs, decision makers, timeline, risks, next steps."

## Marketing

- **Campaign ideas**: "Give me 10 monsoon-season campaign ideas for boutique hotels in Kerala, from safe to bold. Include the target guest and the channel."
- **Content**: "Turn this blog post into 5 LinkedIn posts, 3 Instagram captions, and a newsletter section."
- **SEO**: "Suggest 20 search terms travellers might use to find a pet-friendly stay near Bangalore, grouped by intent."
- **Brand consistency**: build a **Project** or **Skill** (Module 11) with your brand guide so all content matches it.
- **Positioning**: "Here are our homepage and two competitors' [paste]. How are we positioned differently? What's unclear about ours?"

## Revenue, finance, and analytics

- **Data analysis**: upload booking or revenue exports. "Show ADR, occupancy, and RevPAR by month and by channel. What changed most versus last year, and what might explain it?"
- **Spreadsheet help**: "Write an Excel formula that flags bookings where the rate is more than 20% below the seasonal BAR in column F."
- **Forecast thinking**: "Here's our last 2 years of monthly occupancy. Suggest a simple forecasting approach and its limitations."
- **Reporting**: "Turn these numbers into a one-page monthly business review with 3 highlights, 3 concerns, and recommended actions."

*Always cross-check key numbers against your source system before they go to leadership or investors.*

## Product, tech, and design

- **Specs**: "Turn this feature idea into a one-page spec: problem, users, goals, non-goals, user stories, success metrics, open questions."
- **User research**: "Here are 15 interview notes. Synthesise the themes, ranked by frequency and severity, with quotes."
- **Prototypes**: build clickable mock-ups as **Artifacts** to test ideas before engineering time is spent.
- **Engineering**: use **Claude Code** (Module 12) to read, write, test, and review code across your repositories.

## People and hiring

- **Job descriptions**: "Write a JD for a Guest Experience Manager. Make it honest about the pace of a startup and include what success looks like at 90 days."
- **Interview kits**: "Create structured interview questions and a scoring rubric for this role."
- **Feedback**: "Help me turn these notes into clear, kind, specific feedback using the situation-behaviour-impact format."
- **Onboarding**: "Create a 30-60-90 day onboarding plan for a new sales hire."

*Use Claude to structure people processes, not to decide about specific individuals.*

## Founders and leaders

- **Thinking partner**: "Here's our situation and three options [details]. Pressure-test each: what has to be true for it to work, and what are the biggest risks?"
- **Investor updates**: "Turn these metrics and notes into a monthly investor update: highlights, lowlights, metrics table, asks."
- **Board and strategy docs**: "Critique this strategy memo as a board member. What's unclear, unsupported, or missing?"
- **Communication**: "Help me announce [change] to the team. Be honest about why, address likely worries, and keep it under 250 words."

## How to roll this out across a team

1. **Start with one repeated task per person.** Not everything at once.
2. **Build shared Projects** with the team's best prompts, policies, and examples.
3. **Share wins** in a team channel: the prompt, the result, the time saved.
4. **Agree on rules**: what data can go in, what needs human review, what must be disclosed.
5. **Turn proven workflows into Skills or connected workflows** (Track C).

## Try it

1. Pick **two plays from your role** and one from another role, and run them on real work this week.
2. Track time saved in your wins note.
3. Share the best prompt with a colleague.

## Key takeaways

- Every role has high-value plays. Start with the tasks you repeat weekly.
- Projects with policies and examples keep output consistent and on-brand.
- Check numbers and facts before they leave your hands.
- Roll out as a team: shared projects, shared wins, shared rules.
