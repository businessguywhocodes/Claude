# Welcome: Claude 101 and Beyond

This course is for people who have heard a lot about Claude, maybe tried it a few times, and want to get properly good at it. It's written for friends at HotelBFF, so the work examples lean toward hotels, travel, guests, and running a fast-moving company. Everything also applies to personal life.

## Who this is for

- **Complete beginners** who have never used an AI assistant seriously. Start at Module 1 and go in order.
- **Casual users** who use Claude for the odd email and want more out of it. Skim Modules 1–2, then focus on 3, 6, 8 and 9.
- **Power users and builders** who want agents, connectors, and the API. Jump to Track C (Modules 10–13).

## How the course is organised

| Track | Modules | What you'll be able to do |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Foundations (Claude 101)** | 1–5 | Understand what Claude is, set it up, write good prompts, work with files, and use it safely |
| **B. Everyday leverage** | 6–9 | Use Projects, memory, and research; apply Claude across personal life and your job |
| **C. Advanced** | 10–13 | Use advanced prompting, connect Claude to your tools, delegate multi-step work to agents, and build on the API |
| **Capstone** | 14 | A 30-day habit plan and a one-page cheat sheet |

There is also a **[Prompt Library](prompt-library.md)** of copy-paste prompts for common personal and work tasks.

## How to get the most out of it

1. **Do the "Try it" exercises.** Reading about Claude is like reading about swimming. Each module has 2–4 short exercises; they take 5–15 minutes each.
2. **Use your own real tasks.** The fastest way to learn is to bring something you actually need done this week.
3. **Keep a "wins" note.** Each time Claude saves you time or improves your work, write one line about it. After two weeks you'll have a clear picture of where it helps you most.

## A note on accuracy

This course was written in October 2026. Claude's apps change often: features get added, renamed, or moved around in the menus. The ideas here (how to brief Claude, how to check its work, when to use which feature) change slowly. The exact buttons change fast. If something looks different, check [support.claude.com](https://support.claude.com) or just ask Claude: "Where do I find X in the Claude app?"
