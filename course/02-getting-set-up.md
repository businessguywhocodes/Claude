# Module 2: Getting Set Up

**Track A · Foundations · ~20 minutes**

## Where Claude lives

You can reach the same Claude account from several places. Your chats and Projects sync between them.

| Surface | Best for |
|---|---|
| **claude.ai (web)** | Everything; works on any computer |
| **Desktop app (Mac/Windows)** | Daily use, a quick-launch shortcut, local file access, and agent features like Cowork |
| **Mobile app (iOS/Android)** | On the go: voice conversations, photos of documents, quick questions |
| **Claude in Chrome** | Letting Claude read and act on web pages in your browser |
| **Claude in Slack, Excel, PowerPoint and other tools** | Using Claude inside the tools your team already works in |
| **Claude Code** | Software work: reading, writing and running code (Module 12) |
| **The API** | Building Claude into your own products and workflows (Module 13) |

**Recommendation:** install the desktop app and the mobile app. Pin the desktop app and learn its keyboard shortcut so asking Claude becomes as quick as opening a search bar.

## Plans in brief

- **Free**: good for trying Claude out. Lower usage limits and fewer features.
- **Pro / Max**: individual paid plans. Higher limits and access to the most capable models and advanced features. Max is for heavy users.
- **Team / Enterprise**: for companies. Admin controls, shared Projects, connectors managed by the company, and by default your data is not used for model training.

Usage limits reset over time windows. If you hit a limit, wait for the reset, switch to a smaller model, or start fresh chats instead of continuing very long ones (long chats use more of your allowance per message).

> **For work:** check whether HotelBFF has a company Team or Enterprise workspace and use that for work tasks. Keep personal and work accounts separate.

## A tour of the chat screen

The layout changes from time to time, but you'll find these controls:

- **Message box**: where you type. Press Shift+Enter for a new line without sending.
- **Attach (+ or paperclip)**: upload files and images, or add content from connected apps like Google Drive.
- **Model picker**: choose which Claude model to use.
- **Extended thinking**: tells Claude to reason more before answering.
- **Search and tools menu**: turn on web search and Research, and enable connectors.
- **Styles**: change how Claude writes (concise, explanatory, formal, or a custom style you create from your own writing samples).
- **Projects** (sidebar): workspaces with their own instructions and files (Module 6).
- **Artifacts**: the side panel where Claude shows documents, code, and mini-apps it creates (Module 4).
- **Voice**: on mobile and desktop you can talk to Claude and hear answers.
- **Incognito chat**: a chat that isn't saved to your history or used for memory.

## Settings worth five minutes

Open **Settings** and look at:

1. **Profile / personal preferences.** Tell Claude, once, things that should apply to every chat. For example:
   > I work in operations at a hotel-tech startup in India. I prefer concise answers with bullet points, Indian English spelling, and amounts in INR unless I say otherwise. When you're unsure, say so.
2. **Memory.** If available on your plan, decide whether Claude should remember things across chats. You can view and edit what it remembers.
3. **Capabilities / features.** Make sure the features you want, such as artifacts, file creation, web search and code execution, are switched on.
4. **Connectors.** See which apps you can connect (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Slack, Notion and many more). Module 11 covers these properly.
5. **Privacy.** Review the data and training settings so you know how your chats are handled.

## Your first real conversation

Pick something from your actual week, such as an email you've been putting off, a decision you're stuck on, or a document you need to read. Then:

1. **Give context:** who you are, what the situation is, and who it's for.
2. **Say what you want:** a draft, a summary, options, or a critique.
3. **Read the answer critically.**
4. **Reply with feedback**, such as *"shorter"*, *"more direct"*, *"the second option, but warmer"*. Don't start over. Steering is the skill.

## Try it

1. Write your **profile preferences** in Settings (use the example above as a template).
2. Install the **mobile app** and ask Claude something by voice while walking.
3. Take a photo of something on paper, such as a menu, a bill, or a handwritten note, and ask Claude to turn it into a clean table.

## Key takeaways

- Same account everywhere: web, desktop, mobile, browser, and inside your tools.
- Profile preferences save you from repeating yourself in every chat.
- Use the company workspace for work and a personal account for personal life.
- The core skill is iteration: ask, read, steer.
